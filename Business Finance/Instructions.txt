1. Assignment Overview

This is a group assignment worth 20 marks, divided equally:

Question	Topic	Marks
Q1	Dividend Policy – IslandTech Ltd	10
Q2	Acquisition of TechWave Ltd	10
Total		20

The assignment is essentially testing critical thinking in Corporate/Business Finance, rather than simply asking for definitions.

You need to do two things throughout the assignment:

Explain the relevant theories/concepts correctly.
Apply them to the IslandTech–TechWave situation and critically discuss them.

The word "critically" is important. You should not just say "MM theory says X." You should explain:

what the theory says;
why it could apply to IslandTech;
its assumptions/limitations;
what another theory would argue;
and therefore why there is no single universally correct dividend/acquisition decision.
2. Question 1 – Dividend Policy [10 Marks]
Scenario

IslandTech is profitable and has investment opportunities, including the proposed acquisition of TechWave.

One director says:

If IslandTech can invest its earnings profitably, it should retain the money rather than pay dividends because shareholders will benefit from future growth.

You must critically evaluate this statement using four dividend-policy theories.

Required aspects

You must cover all four:

1. MM Dividend Irrelevance Theory

Explain that under the Modigliani–Miller approach, dividend policy itself does not affect shareholder wealth under certain assumptions.

Then discuss whether those assumptions realistically apply to IslandTech.

2. Dividend Signalling Theory

Explain that dividends can communicate information about management's expectations regarding future earnings/cash flows.

For IslandTech:

maintaining/increasing dividends may signal confidence;
reducing dividends may be interpreted negatively;
retaining earnings could also be justified if management has credible profitable investment opportunities.
3. Agency Theory

Discuss the conflict between:

managers;
shareholders.

Retaining too much cash gives management more funds under its control, potentially creating agency problems. Paying dividends can reduce excess cash available for managerial misuse.

But retaining earnings can also reduce dependence on external financing for genuinely profitable investments.

4. Bird-in-the-Hand Argument

Explain the argument that investors may prefer a certain current dividend over uncertain future capital gains.

Then critically discuss its limitations and contrast it with MM's view.

Q1 should ultimately connect back to IslandTech

The question is specifically asking you to show that the theories can lead to different conclusions.

For example:

Retain earnings
→ finance profitable investment/acquisition
→ potentially increase future growth and shareholder wealth.

But:

Pay dividends
→ provide shareholders with immediate returns
→ potentially reduce agency problems
→ may signal financial strength.

So the final discussion should not simply say "IslandTech should retain earnings."

Instead, explain the conditions under which retention or dividend payment may be appropriate.

3. Question 2 – Acquisition [10 Marks]
Scenario

IslandTech wants to acquire TechWave.

One director argues:

IslandTech will become larger, gain market share and achieve economies of scale, therefore shareholder wealth will increase.

You need to critically assess whether this reasoning is actually valid.

Required aspects
1. Main Motives for Acquisition

Explain why IslandTech might acquire TechWave, such as:

economies of scale;
increased market share;
revenue growth;
access to customers/markets;
operational efficiencies;
access to technology or expertise;
diversification, where relevant;
potential synergies.

Then connect these motives to the IslandTech–TechWave case.

2. Sources of Synergy

Explain what synergy means:

The combined business can potentially create more value than the two companies could create separately.

You can discuss:

Operating synergies

cost savings;
elimination of duplicated functions;
economies of scale;
improved efficiency.

Revenue synergies

cross-selling;
access to TechWave's customers;
increased sales;
stronger market position.

Financial synergies

potentially lower financing costs;
better access to capital;
more efficient use of financial resources.
3. Risk of Overpaying

This is a major critical point.

Even if TechWave genuinely has valuable synergies, IslandTech can destroy shareholder wealth if it pays too much for those expected benefits.

For example:

Expected value from TechWave + expected synergies = MUR X

But if IslandTech pays significantly more than the value created, the acquisition may transfer much of the expected benefit to TechWave's shareholders rather than IslandTech's shareholders.

Therefore:

Synergy ≠ guaranteed shareholder wealth creation.

4. Integration & Financing Risks

Discuss difficulties such as:

different organisational cultures;
employee resistance;
loss of key employees;
incompatible systems;
management difficulties;
unexpected integration costs;
disruption to normal operations.

Then discuss financing:

If IslandTech uses debt:

interest obligations increase;
financial risk increases;
cash flows may become more constrained.

If it uses new equity:

existing shareholders may experience dilution;
issuing shares can affect ownership/control.

Therefore, the method of financing matters.

5. Size/Market Share ≠ Shareholder Wealth

This is the key critical evaluation.

A larger company is not automatically a more valuable company.

Similarly:

Higher market share does not automatically mean higher shareholder wealth.

The acquisition creates shareholder wealth only if the value created exceeds the total cost of acquiring and integrating TechWave, including the acquisition premium and financing/integration costs.

Therefore, the director's argument is incomplete.
