Suggested Word/Page Allocation

Since the maximum is 6 pages excluding cover, TOC and references, you need to control the length.

A reasonable target:

Section	Approx. space
Introduction	¼ page
Q1 – MM + Signalling	~1 page
Q1 – Agency + Bird-in-the-Hand + comparison	~1 page
Q2 – Motives + Synergies	~1 page
Q2 – Overpayment + Integration	~1 page
Q2 – Financing + Wealth Creation	~1 page
Overall transitions/conclusions	~½ page
Total	~5¾ pages

Don't try to fill six pages with unnecessary theory. Quality of analysis matters more than simply reaching six pages.
