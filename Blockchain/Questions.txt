1. Architecture Choice (8 Marks)

Should the University of Mauritius adopt a public permissionless blockchain (e.g., public Ethereum or Polygon) or a private permissioned blockchain (e.g., a consortium network hosted across public universities in Mauritius like UoM, UTM, and Open University of Mauritius using Hyperledger Fabric)?

Justify your recommended architecture considering:

Privacy: Protecting student records under local compliance standards.

Cost: Handling gas fees or node maintenance infrastructure within a university budget in Mauritian Rupees (MUR).

Trust & Verifiability: Ensuring global employers and domestic institutions can independently trust the degree records without relying on UoM's IT servers remaining online forever.








2. Workflow & Verification Process (10 Marks)

Outline the end-to-end workflow for issuing and verifying a UoM digital degree. Describe the step-by-step procedure for:

Issuance: How the UoM Examinations Office signs and issues the graduate's credential.

On-Chain Recording: What exact data or cryptographic proof gets committed to the ledger.

Verification: How a HR manager at a company in Ébène Cybercity or an international university verifies the authenticity of the candidate's degree in seconds without contacting the UoM Registry.








3. Privacy & Mauritian Regulatory Compliance (7 Marks)

Under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 (DPA); which heavily aligns with the European Union's GDPR (citizens have the Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") managed by the Data Protection Office (DPO) of Mauritius.)

Explain how the immutable, unalterable nature of blockchain conflicts with the DPA 2017 requirements if a student requests the deletion of their personal educational records.

Propose an architectural solution combining off-chain data storage (e.g., decentralized storage or secure UoM databases) and on-chain cryptographic hashes to ensure strict DPA compliance while preserving the degree's tamper-proof verifiability.
