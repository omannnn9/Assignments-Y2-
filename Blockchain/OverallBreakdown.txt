First: Understand the scenario

The University of Mauritius wants to replace/reduce reliance on traditional paper/PDF degrees.
Currently, imagine a graduate applies for a job at a company in Ebène Cybercity.

The company has to ask:
"Is this person's degree actually genuine?"

Traditionally, the employer might contact UoM or rely on a certificate that could potentially be forged.

Blockchain solution

UoM could issue a digital degree credential.

For example:

Bhaav Moorut
Bachelor of Science (Hons) FinTech
University of Mauritius
Awarded: 2026

Instead of putting all this personal information directly onto a blockchain, UoM can create a cryptographic hash of the credential and store that proof on blockchain.

Then:

Graduate → gives digital degree → Employer → verifies blockchain proof → Genuine / Not genuine

The important idea is:

Blockchain proves that the credential has not been altered since it was issued.
