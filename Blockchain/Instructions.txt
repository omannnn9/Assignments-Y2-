Assignment Submission Guidelines & Instructions
1. General Logistics & Submission Details
● Submission Deadline: Week 7 (20th September 2026) by 16:00 (Local Mauritius
Time)
● Submission Mode: Electronic submission via UoM Google Classroom
● Cover Page Requirement: Every submission MUST include the standard UoM
Assignment Cover Sheet (ACS) completely filled in with:
○ Students Names & Student ID (ID Number)
○ Programme of Study (e.g., BSc (Hons) Computer Science, BSc (Hons) Applied
Computing)
○ Module Code & Module Title
○ Lecturer/Tutor Name
2. Submission Format & Word Count
● File Format: A single PDF document ([StudentID]_[ModuleCode]_Assignment1.pdf).
● Formatting Requirements:
○ Font: Times New Roman (12 pt) or Arial (11 pt)
○ Line Spacing: 1.5 lines
○ Margins: Standard 1 inch (2.54 cm) on all sides
○ Page Numbers: Bottom center or bottom right (e.g., Page X of Y)
● Word Count:
○ Part A (Theory & Analysis): 800 – 1,200 words .
○ Part B (Practical System Design): Diagrams, architectural workflows, and
structured explanatory text.

3. Referencing & Academic Integrity (UoM Standards)
● Referencing Style: You must strictly follow the UoM Harvard System of Referencing
for all in-text citations and the concluding Reference List.

○ In-Text Citation Example: According to local financial regulations (Bank of
Mauritius 2024)... or (Nakamoto 2008, p. 3).
○ Reference List Format: Must be arranged alphabetically by author surname at the
end of the assignment.
● Plagiarism & Generative AI Policy:
○ All submissions will be passed through Turnitin. A similarity index exceeding
20% (excluding references) or clear evidence of unacknowledged AI generation
will be subjected to the UoM Disciplinary Committee for academic misconduct.

4. Late Submission Penalties (UoM Regulations)
In accordance with University of Mauritius Senate regulations:
● Late Submissions: Assignments submitted after the deadline without prior approved
extension by the Lecturer/Head of Department will incur a deduction of 5% of total
allocated marks per working day late.
● Submissions delayed by more than 5 working days past the Week 7 deadline will
receive a mark of 0% (Grade F).
