CASE STUDY
MyLib — Public Library Information System

MyLib is a public library which serves two types of visitors: Members who come for
walk-in services and members who come on pre-booked study room reservations.
Walk-in services are available from 08.00 am to 20.00 pm Monday to Saturday, while
study room reservations are available from 09.00 am to 17.00 pm Monday to Friday.
When a visitor arrives for walk-in services, he goes to the front desk to register using his
National Identity Card (NIC). He is asked for his name, address, date of birth and
contact number and is given a membership card. After registration, members are
required to speak to the Library Assistant who asks them the purpose of their visit to the
library. The Library Assistant makes a note on the member’s card and directs the
member to the appropriate section.
When a Librarian attends to a member, he looks at the note made by the Library
Assistant and checks the member’s request. He then processes the request: If the
member wishes to borrow a book that is not available on the shelves, the Librarian
checks the catalogue system to determine if the book is available at another branch. If
available at another branch, the Librarian initiates an Inter-Library Loan (ILL) request
with a note specifying the book details, otherwise he suggests alternative titles to the
member.
For members requiring an inter-library loan, after the request is processed and after
receiving confirmation from the other branch, the Librarian will notify the member of the
expected collection date. If the book is unavailable at all branches, the Librarian will
place a reservation and notify the member when the book becomes available.
If the member wishes to borrow a book available on the shelves, the Librarian scans the
book and the membership card to record the loan. At the end of a visit, if a member
needs to collect an inter-library loan book or has a reservation, the Librarian will put a
note on the member’s card and ask him to go back to the front desk to book a collection
appointment.
Members attending pre-booked study room reservations need to go to the front desk to
notify the receptionist that they have arrived. After checking the name of the person in
the reservation book, the receptionist provides the member with a room key and directs
him to the assigned study room. After the session, the member returns the key to the
front desk. If the member needs a follow-up reservation, he needs to go to the front desk
again to make a new booking.
Whenever a member comes to the front desk for a reservation, the receptionist asks for
his NIC and the preferred date, time and room type (individual or group study room).
The receptionist will then verify in the reservation book the availability of the room and
propose a reservation day and time to the member. Once the reservation is agreed
upon, the receptionist notes it down alongside the member’s name and contact number.
Members can also book a study room by calling the library or by sending an email to the
front desk.
