MyLib Assignment — Simple Guide
1. What do we have to do?

This assignment is about analysing how the MyLib public library currently works and creating diagrams to represent its processes.

The assignment is 40 marks in total and contributes 16% of the course mark.

Main tasks

Study Room Process Diagram	    7 Marks   - in Group Report

--------------------------------------------

All diagrams must be created using ARIS




2. What are the 3 diagrams?
A. Collaboration Diagram — 5 marks

This shows:

WHO interacts with WHO?

Main people/systems:

Member
Receptionist / Front Desk
Library Assistant
Librarian
Catalogue System
Other Library Branch
Reservation Book/System

Show the messages/interactions between them and their sequence.





3. Walk-in Process Diagram — 10 marks

This is the most important diagram.

Show the complete journey of a member who walks into the library.

Basic flow:

Member arrives

↓

Register using NIC

↓

Enter name, address, DOB, contact

↓

Receive membership card

↓

Library Assistant asks purpose

↓

Member goes to Librarian

↓

Librarian processes request

Then you have different possibilities:

Book available on shelf

Scan book + membership card → Record loan

Book not available

Check catalogue

→ Available at another branch?

YES → Inter-Library Loan → Confirmation → Notify member

NO → Reserve book → Notify member when available

The assignment specifically requires registration, Library Assistant assessment, Librarian processing, ILL and reservation to be considered.




4. Study Room Process Diagram — 7 marks

Show how a member books and uses a study room.

Booking:

Member wants room

↓

Can book by:

Front desk
Phone
Email

↓

Receptionist asks for:

NIC
Date
Time
Room type

↓

Check reservation book

↓

Is room available?

YES → Confirm and record reservation

NO → Offer another date/time

On the actual day:

Member arrives

↓

Receptionist checks reservation

↓

Give room key

↓

Member uses room

↓

Returns key

↓

Needs another booking?

YES → Make new reservation

NO → END

These steps come directly from the case study.





5. Three Weaknesses + Improvements — 3 marks

Give 3 problems with the current manual system and explain how MyLib could improve them.

Example:
Weakness	                                       Improvement
Manual reservation book	                  Digital booking system
Notes written on membership cards	        Digital member records
Staff manually notify members	            Automatic SMS/email notifications

Keep these specific to MyLib.


6. Group Report — 25 marks

Your report should basically contain:


1. Introduction

2. Collaboration Diagram

3. Walk-in Process Diagram

4. Study Room Process Diagram

5. 3 Weaknesses + Improvements

6. Conclusion

The three diagrams are the most important part because they account for 22 of the 25 Group Report marks.



7. Presentation — 5 marks

You have a maximum of 15 minutes.

Keep it simple:

Introduce MyLib
Explain Collaboration Diagram
Explain Walk-in Process
Explain Study Room Process
Explain weaknesses + improvements
Conclusion

Don't read everything from the slides. Explain your diagrams.





8. Individual Reflection — 5 marks

Each student submits one page individually.

Simply discuss:

What I contributed
What I learned
What difficulties I faced
What I would improve / lessons learned
