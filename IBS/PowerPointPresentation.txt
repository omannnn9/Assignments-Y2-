MyLib — Presentation & Individual Reflection Breakdown

The idea should be simple: each person presents the part they worked on and writes about their own contribution in the individual reflective report.

The presentation is 5 marks, while each student's reflective report is 5 marks individually.

1. Presentation — 15 Minutes

I recommend giving each member approximately 3 minutes.

Member	Presentation Topic	Time
Ashfarh	Collaboration Diagram	~3 min
Oman	Walk-in Process Diagram	~3 min
Farhaan	Study Room Process Diagram	~3 min
Yohann	Introduction + Weaknesses & Improvements	~3 min
Aayush	Overall Report/Conclusion + Summary	~3 min
	TOTAL	~15 min

This keeps everyone involved rather than having one person do most of the presentation.
