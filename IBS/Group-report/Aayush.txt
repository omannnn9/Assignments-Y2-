The collaboration diagram illustrates the communication bet MyLib and the parties involved in its services. This included 5 pools, whereby the Member is the visitor, and MyLib is the library, split into 3 lanes: Front Desk, Library Assistant and Librarian. There are also, 3 outside parties involved in the process: the Catalogue system, the Other Library Branch and the Reservation system.

The interactions are illustrated using the dotted message flows, which are numbered from 1 to 21, to make the order easy to follow.

So, for the Walk-in services (Monday - Saturday), the member fills in the registration details (1) and receives a membership card (2). The member then tells the Library Assistant the purpose of his visit (3) and is then provided with a note on the card and directions upon his requirement (4). For the next step, the member provides his book request (5) to the Librarian, who will verify the Catalogue System (6 & 7). If the book is at another branch, the Librarian sends an Inter-Library Loan (ILL) request (8) and receives a confirmation (9). Following this, the Librarian then gives the member the result of the process or provide alternatives for the book (10). And finally, If there is a demand for a particular collection, the member will request a collection appointment at the Front Desk (11).

Additionally, for the Study Rooms (Monday - Friday), Bookings are welcomed through walk-in, phone o email. So, the member will send a booking request (12), which the Front Desk will verify via the Registration Book (13 & 14) and then will propose a date and time (15). Once the member agrees, the booking is recorded (16). On the visiting day, the member provides an arrival notice (17), the Front Desk cross-checks it with the Reservation Book (18) and gives the room key and directions to follow (19). Finally, when the session is completed, the key is returned (20), and the member can request a follow-up reservation (21), which therefore loops back to recording a booking.

Overall, the diagram illustrates how communication is being done between the parties and in what specific order, and the process diagrams shows the detailed steps.
