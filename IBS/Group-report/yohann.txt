Introduction
Our report analyses different process of the MyLib Public Library Information System. This library information system is a public library that provides services to members through walk-in library services as well as pre-booked study room reservations. The case study explores how each members interact with the library staff and systems to access books, reserve study rooms and receive other library services.
The purpose of our report is to model and evaluate the key processes of MyLib using the appropriate diagrams and process analysis techniques. Our report includes a Collaboration Diagram illustrating the interactions between participants, a Walk-in Member Services Process Diagram, and a Pre-Booked Study Room Reservation Process Diagram. Our diagram models provides a clear understanding of information required, responsibilities of staff members, and the services offered to library members.
In addition, our report identifies all weaknesses within the current library operations and provides improvements that can be done through the MyLib Information System to enhance efficiency, service quality, and the overall member experience.


Conclusion
After analysing the MyLib Public Library Information System, our diagrams and report demonstrates how the library manages both walk-in services and pre-booked study room reservations through a series of coordinated interactions between members, reception staff, library assistants, librarians, and supporting systems. The diagrams developed by our teamin this report provide a clear representation of these processes and highlight the series of activities involved in delivering library services. 
Our evaluation of the current processes showed that in several areas efficiency and service quality could be improved, especially in member registration, study room reservations, and access to book availability information. By implementing digital solutions such as online registration, electronic reservation systems, and integrated catalogue services, MyLib can simplify its operations, reduce manual work, and provide a more convenient experience for its users and members.
Overall, the proposed improvements would support better resource management, faster service delivery, and enhanced customer satisfaction while contributing to the long-term effectiveness of the MyLib Public Library's information system.
