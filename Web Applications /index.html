About Us

XYZ Master is a professional home and property services company based in Port Louis, Mauritius. We provide reliable maintenance and improvement solutions for homes and businesses.

Our services include home cleaning, plumbing, electrical work, painting and decoration, garden and landscaping, and general property maintenance.

We are committed to professionalism, quality service and customer satisfaction. Our goal is to provide practical solutions that help customers maintain and improve their properties.
