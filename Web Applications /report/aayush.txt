Contact Page

Design
My contribution to the XYZ Master website was through the development of the Contact page, which allows customers to reach out to the business for any assistance they need. The design was mostly kept the same in terms and visual interface as the rest of the website to keep the Contact page feels like part of the same website, and not a separate page. Our team considered this consistency very important since a professional-looking and user-friendly website builds trust with potential customers, and thus a inconsistent work can make both the business and its website look unreliable.
The form was designed to gather the main information from a customer in order to provide the appropriate assistance to their queries. These involved their name, email address, password, phone number, type of service they want (dropdown), their preferred contact method (radio buttons), and so on. Each field type was chosen for a specific reason. A dropdown restricts the customer to valid service options, radio buttons limit the customer to one choice where only one answer is valid, while checkboxes allow multiples selections where more than one answer is valid. As such the variety for the codes used proves the practical use of the different HTML form elements that can be integrated for the development of a website like XYZ Master.

Operation
The “Contact” section plays a key role in any business website, since it the medium through which a visitor becomes a potential customer. If the procedures of contacting the business confusing, broken, unreliable or not even user-friendly, the business risks losing enquiries entirely. Therefore, for this reason, the form’s section was built around client-side validation using JavaScript.
Before any submission of a form, JavaScript verifies each field. It ensures no required field is left empty, confirms if the email address is correctly formatted, validates password meeting the minimum required length, checks that the phone number contains only digits, and ensures at least one contact-time and once service option are selected. If any verification contradicts, an alert message immediately notifies the customer where a rectification is required, and thus preventing the form from submitting until all information is valid.
This mechanism matters because it improves the quality of the data the business receives (no empty or unusable enquiries) and improves the customer’s experience by being a user-friendly and easy to use website.
Therefore, as an overall, JavaScript was the appropriate website language for this because it runs directly in the browser, allowing real-time feedback without needing to reload the page or wait for a server response.
